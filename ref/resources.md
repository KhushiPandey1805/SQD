## What this project does (for the novelty comparison; description of our own work, not of the literature)

1. Task: ground-state energy of an assigned 8-qubit Jordan-Wigner **Pauli-list Hamiltonian** of H2O, CAS(4e,4o), cc-pVQZ (CASSCF reference -76.11669094458742 Ha), supplied **without** the one-/two-electron integrals (qubit-Hamiltonian SQD route, `qiskit_addon_sqd.qubit`).
2. State preparation: LUCJ circuit (ffsim) with **local** interaction pairs, initialised from supplied **CCSD t1/t2 amplitudes** via double factorization, then **variationally minimised against the qubit Hamiltonian** over all LUCJ parameters (n_reps = 3 and 4).
3. Sampling: noiseless simulator and IBM hardware (ibm_phoenix), 10,000 shots, two-qubit depth 51 (r3) and 65 (r4).
4. Post-processing: standard SQD (self-consistent configuration recovery, probability-weighted subsampling, subspace diagonalisation), subspace-size sweep 5-36 determinants with mean and spread over 10 post-processing seeds.
5. Reported figures: variational error ~0.37 mHa after minimisation (from 38.8 mHa for the raw seed); hardware SQD mean error 1.45 mHa (r3) / 1.89 mHa (r4) at 25 determinants and 0.38 / 0.15 mHa at 30 (of 36 total determinants).
6. Planned: same procedure for H2 and triplet O2, reaction energy dE = 2E(H2) + E(O2) - 2E(H2O).
